# MIMO Partner Brand Guidelines v1.0

**Effective 22 October 2026 · Version 1.0 · MassLogic Labs, Inc.**

*Attached by reference to the MIMO Partner Agreement as "Company Brand Guidelines."*

---

## 1. Names

**Company.** Write **MassLogic Labs**. Use the full legal name **MassLogic Labs, Inc.** in footers and legal lockups only.

**Platform.** Write **MIMO**, always in capitals. MIMO stands alone in headlines. Do not expand the acronym.

| Correct | Incorrect |
|---|---|
| MassLogic Labs | MassLogic, Masslogic, MassLogic LLC |
| MIMO | Mimo, mimo (in text) |
| MIMO Workspace | MIMO workspace, Workspace by MIMO |

The MIMO logo is set in lowercase as "mimo". That applies to the logotype only. In written text MIMO is always capitals.

**Products you may name.** MIMO Workspace, MIMO Intelligence, MIMO Marketspace, MIMO Canvas, Brand Radar, Brand Radar Diagnostic, Brand Radar Wave, Brand Monitoring.

**MIMO Orchestration is a layer, not a product.** Do not list it as something a customer buys.

**Trademark symbols.** Use ™ on these marks and no others:

> MIMO™ · MIMO Workspace™ · MIMO Intelligence™ · MIMO Orchestration™ · MIMO Marketspace™ · Brand Radar™ · MassLogic Labs™

Place ™ on the first or most prominent mention on a page. Later mentions in the same document drop it.

**Do not add ™ to MIMO Canvas.** Canvas is a surface, not a sold product.

**Brand Radar™ is the mark.** "Diagnostic" and "Wave" are descriptors, not separate marks. Write Brand Radar™ Diagnostic on first mention, then Diagnostic.

**Attribution.** Include this where you carry a footer:

> © MassLogic Labs, Inc. 2026. MIMO™, MIMO Workspace™, MIMO Intelligence™, MIMO Orchestration™, MIMO Marketspace™, Brand Radar™, and MassLogic Labs™ are trademarks of MassLogic Labs, Inc. All other trademarks are the property of their respective owners.

## 2. How to describe MIMO

**Short, one sentence:**

> MIMO is the intelligence engine for enterprise marketing.

**Long, two to three sentences:**

> MIMO is your brand's intelligence at the center of your AI stack. Owned by you, governing every AI that touches your brand. The MarTech stack manages branding. MIMO governs brand.

Use these as written. Do not paraphrase them into new claims.

## 3. Never say

| Do not say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| MIMO is neutral, vendor-neutral, unbiased or independent | MIMO is vendor-agnostic, or "governs every AI, regardless of vendor" |
| MIMO is a MarTech tool or platform | The stack manages branding. MIMO governs brand. |
| MIMO is governance software, or governance and privacy software | MIMO is how brands run AI Brand Governance |
| Brand Radar governs | Brand Radar measures and flags |
| Brand Signal, Brand Intelligence (as a product name), AI Brand Intelligence | Brand Radar |
| Brand Brain | MIMO Intelligence |
| Marketplace | MIMO Marketspace |
| MIMO Prime | Not a product. Do not use. |
| MIMO replaces AI visibility, GEO, AEO or reputation management | Those are inputs MIMO governs |

**Three more prohibitions.**

- **Standards bodies.** MASB is the only standards partner you may name. Do not name ANA, AMA or any other body as a current partner.
- **Other vendors.** Do not name an AI visibility or adjacent-category vendor alongside MIMO. Write "your existing AI visibility platform".
- **Your advisory lane.** If you provide vendor-neutral advisory services, do not present MIMO as part of them, inside them, or endorsed by them. Keep the two separate in all materials.

Do not brand "Marketing Operating Model" or "MOM" as a MIMO product. It is a general industry term and is fine in that sense only.

## 4. Logos and visual identity

**Colors.** Navy `#001937`. Gold `#D9A431`. Stone `#F7ECD4`. These three only. Do not use `#0D1B2A`, `#C9A227` or `#E0E1DD`.

**Typefaces.** Inter Variable for interface and body. Source Serif 4 for pull quotes and long form.

**Which mark to use.**

- Light backgrounds: the primary wordmark, navy brain with gold wordmark.
- Dark backgrounds: the secondary wordmark, stone brain with gold wordmark.
- Icon only: the approved brain and circuit mark.

**Clear space.** At least the width of the inner "M" on all sides.

**Minimum size.** Full lockup 120px wide. Icon only 96px wide.

**Do not:** recolor, rotate, skew, stretch, add shadows or gradients, redraw or reconstruct any mark. Do not place a mark on a gradient background.

**Where to get assets.** Request current files from brand@masslogiclabs.com. A partner-facing location is in progress.

## 5. Co-branding

- You may show your logo beside a MIMO mark in materials describing work you deliver.
- Keep both marks at equal visual weight. Observe the clear space in section 4.
- **Do not suggest a joint product.** There is no joint product.
- **Do not suggest a jointly owned index, score, benchmark or research program.**
- Do not lock your mark to a MIMO mark, or create a combined logo.
- All co-branded materials need approval before publication. See section 7.

## 6. Customer references

You may not do any of the following without written consent from both MassLogic Labs and the customer:

- Name a joint customer.
- Use a joint customer's logo.
- Publish a case study, testimonial or result that identifies a joint customer.

This applies to your website, decks, social posts, award entries and press.

## 7. Approval

**Get written approval before you publish:**

- Press releases and media comment that mention MIMO or MassLogic Labs.
- Case studies and customer stories.
- Co-branded materials of any kind.
- Paid media using MIMO or MassLogic Labs names or marks.
- Any claim about what MIMO does or how well it performs.

**Who approves.** MassLogic Labs, at brand@masslogiclabs.com.

**How long.** Three business days from receipt, for MassLogic Labs' own review. Where third-party consent is also required, such as a customer's consent under section 6, that time is additional and sits outside this period.

No response within three business days does not mean approval. Do not publish until approval is given in writing.

## 8. Updates

MassLogic Labs may update these guidelines on notice. Notice is given by email to your registered partner contact, with the updated version posted at https://masslogiclabs.com/brand-guidelines. Changes take effect 30 days after notice unless the notice states otherwise.
